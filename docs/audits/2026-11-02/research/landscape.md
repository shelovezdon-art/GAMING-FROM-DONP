# The Roblox Sports-Game Landscape — November 2026

Research date: 2 November 2026. Player figures are concurrent-user (CCU) counts.
Roblox CCU is volatile and third-party trackers disagree; I flag disagreements
rather than averaging them.

---

## 0. The thing to absorb first: the platform itself is contracting

**VERIFIED, primary source.** Roblox's own Q3 2026 shareholder letter
([Q3 2026 Earnings Shareholder Letter](https://s27.q4cdn.com/984876518/files/content_files/Q3-2026-Earnings-Shareholder-Letter.pdf)):

| Metric | Q3 2026 | Change |
|---|---|---|
| DAU | 138 million | **down 9% YoY** |
| Bookings | $1,697M | **down 12% YoY** (vs $1,922M Q3 2025) |
| Hours engaged | ~34 billion | **down 15% YoY**, up 17% QoQ |
| DevEx fees paid to creators | $403M | **down 6% YoY** (vs $428M Q3 2025) |

This is the first time in ALLSTAR WORLD's planning history that the platform is
shrinking rather than growing. It does not make the project a bad idea — 138M DAU
is still an enormous market and the creator pool is paid $403M a quarter — but it
kills one specific assumption: **you cannot count on platform growth to carry a
mediocre launch.** Share has to be taken from incumbents.

The one bright figure is hours up 17% quarter-over-quarter, which suggests the
decline may be flattening. One quarter is not a trend.

### American football specifically — the sub-genre is weaker than the leaders

This matters most for you, since football is your first vertical.

- **Football Fusion 2** — **VERIFIED** on Rolimons
  ([Football Fusion 2](https://www.rolimons.com/game/8204899140)):
  1.71 **billion** visits, 910,538 favourites, 655,338 likes vs 125,539 dislikes

unaddressed — the single clearest opening on this list.

## 2. What the "realistic upcoming football" tier is actually doing

**This is where I have to be honest about a verification limit.** The owner's
reference set comes from Instagram reels, and those projects are mostly
unreleased, unindexed and promoted through short video. I could not verify their
internals from primary sources. What I found:

- **Named, in-development projects I could locate:**
  **WF — World Futbol**, recruiting on the DevForum
  ([WF — World Futbol](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/wf-world-futbol/4774051));
  an unnamed football project seeking feedback
  ([FOOTBALL game feedback needed](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/football-game-feedback-needed/4853998));
  **Road to Glory: Football (Beta)**, a career-mode game
  ([Roblox](https://www.roblox.com/games/120458890713784/Road-to-Glory-Football));
  **RFX**, marketed on TikTok as "the new football game coming to Roblox in 2026"
  ([@joinrfx](https://www.tiktok.com/@joinrfx/video/7695426740230868232));
  **FOOTBALL DREAM 2026** ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MW0bw6b7X90)).
- **Real-time motion capture is being used by hobbyists**, which explains the jump
  in animation quality in those reels
  ([[Showcase] Real-Time Motion Capture in Roblox](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/showcase-real-time-motion-capture-in-roblox-looking-for-feedback/3250296)).

**What I could NOT verify:** their ball-control models, camera rigs, animation
sourcing, match formats or mobile handling. None of it is published. **Do not let
anyone, including me, tell you what that tier is "doing" mechanically.** A reel is
a marketing artefact, chosen to look good; you cannot infer from it how possession
is resolved or whether it holds 60 FPS on a phone.

**The actionable warning in that gap:** a vertical-slice reel is cheap and a
shipped networked sports game is not. Some of that tier will never ship. Judging
your progress against their *reels* is judging a product against a trailer. Judge
against **Realistic Street Soccer's 40,163 concurrent** — a shipped game whose
reputation is literally "realistic animations".

### Where realism actually ranks

Look at what wins versus what is praised for realism:

- The #1 sports game (**Volleyball Legends**, 151K) is known for a **two-button
  control scheme**.
- #2 (**Blue Lock: Rivals**, 148K) is an **anime licence-adjacent ability game**.
- #3 (**Illegal Soccer**, 81K) is soccer **with jetpacks**.
- #4 (**Basketball: Zero**, 54K) is known for **simple shooting controls**.
- The game explicitly known for realism (**Realistic Street Soccer**) sits at
  **40K** — strong, but a quarter of the leader.

**Realism is a differentiator in this market, not the winning axis.** The top of
the chart is won by *legibility* — a control scheme a 12-year-old masters on a
phone in one match — and by *character identity*. The owner's instinct toward
realistic, 2K-style feel is a genuine wedge, but it must be delivered through a
control scheme as legible as a two-button volleyball game, or it will lose to one.

## 3. What appears to drive retention

### The hard benchmark numbers — **VERIFIED**

From [2026 Roblox Benchmark Report, GameAnalytics](https://www.gameanalytics.com/reports/2026-roblox-report)
(median of 500+ titles with 1M+ MAU, Aug 2025–Jul 2026):

| Metric | p25 | Median | p90 | p99 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 retention | 8.4% | **10.3%** | — | 22.2% |
| D7 retention | 1.0% | **1.6%** | — | 9.1% |
| D30 retention | 0.3% | **0.5%** | — | 4.7% |
| Session length (min) | 7.3 | **9.8** | 14.7 | 21.1 |
| Sessions/player/day | 1.34 | **1.56** | 3.22 | 8.30 |
| ARPPU | $0.32 | **$0.70** | $2.33 | $55.31 |

Platform-wide, **3.80% of tracked players paid anything at all** over the period.

**Calibrate on these.** Median D1 is 10.3% and median D30 is 0.5%. If your
internal targets assume 40% D1, they are not ambitious, they are wrong, and every
downstream revenue projection built on them is wrong too. Note that Football
Fusion 2's **7.01-minute average playtime**
([Rolimons](https://www.rolimons.com/game/8204899140)) sits *below* the p25
session length of 7.3 minutes — a billion-visit sports game converts a session
worse than the median Roblox game.

### What the leaders have in common (my read, from the mechanical descriptions)

1. **Shipping cadence is the retention mechanic.** Football Fusion 2 holds 1.7B
   lifetime visits and ~1,600 concurrent players, last updated 8 months ago.
   Blue Lock: Rivals showed "Updated: 1 min ago" when I checked and holds ~148K.
   The clearest signal in this dataset is that **the genre punishes dormancy
   faster than it rewards quality.**
2. **Named identities and classes.** Blue Lock: Rivals, Basketball: Zero and
   Azure Latch all lead with character classes carrying distinct abilities.
   Players return for *a character they main*, not for a physics model.
3. **A ranked ladder beside a casual mode.** Volleyball Legends runs separate
   pickup and ranked modes; Blue Lock: Rivals has a separate `[RANKED]` place
   ([RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/games/7738294685/blue-lock-rivals-ranked)).
   Casual acquires, ranked retains.
4. **Small servers.** Football Fusion 2's max server size is **14**
   ([Rolimons](https://www.rolimons.com/game/8204899140)); the leaders are 4v4,
   5v5, 6v6. Nobody is winning this genre with 22-a-side. Small servers mean
   shorter queues, less networking load and less bandwidth per match.
5. **Cosmetic identity as the earner** — see `monetization.md`.

## 4. Where the openings genuinely are

**Opening 1 — the abandoned American-football audience. This is the real one.**
Football Fusion 2 has 1.71B visits and 910K favourites and is effectively dormant
at ~1,600 concurrent. FF3 launched to 12,954. NFL Universe Football holds the
licensed, officially-branded position at ~38K and cannot be out-licensed by a
solo studio. The gap is **the unlicensed, mechanically serious American football
game that ships every week.** It does not require beating the NFL licence; it
requires being the thing a lapsed Fusion player moves to.

**Opening 2 — control legibility at realistic fidelity.** Nobody in the top tier
pairs realistic animation with a two-button-legible scheme. Realistic Street
Soccer has the animation and 40K players; Volleyball Legends has the scheme and
151K. That intersection is unoccupied, and it is exactly where a 2K-style dribble
mapped to one stick and one button lands (see `dribbling.md`).

**Opening 3 — first-mover on first-party server authority.** Roblox shipped
Server Authority to full release on 9 July 2026 **with a Soccer template**
([Full Release](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/full-release-ship-fair-and-competitive-games-with-server-authority/4727993)).
The incumbents were all built before it on client-authoritative ball ownership,
which is exploitable and inconsistent (see `technical.md` §1, §6). "Tackles and
possession that are the same on both screens, and cannot be hacked" is a claim no
incumbent can currently make, and it is now available to you as a platform
feature rather than a six-month networking project.

**Opening 4 — football *then* soccer, in that order, is the right sequencing.**
Soccer on Roblox is crowded (Blue Lock: Rivals, Illegal Soccer, Realistic Street
Soccer, FIFA Super Soccer, Azure Latch, Turbo Soccer, That Freekick Challenge all
above 20K). American football has one dormant giant, one weak successor and one
licensed incumbent. **Your stated order — football first — attacks the soft target
first.** Keep it.

### Where the openings are NOT

- **Not in being more realistic than the reels.** Realism is table stakes for
  credibility and not the growth axis. See §2.
- **Not in a bigger match format.** The market has settled on 4v4–6v6.
- **Not in breadth.** Shipping football, soccer and basketball thinly loses to one
  game updated weekly. The data in §3 says cadence beats scope. Build football
  until it holds a ranked population, then add a sport.
- **Not in licensed branding.** Voldex holds the NFL relationship.

## 5. The identity line — read this before building

Mechanics are fair game. Identity is not. Specifically:

- **Do not reuse any of these games' assets, characters, team names, logos,
  marks, UI or overall look.** The NFL team names, logos and stadiums in NFL
  Universe Football are **licensed** to Voldex and the NFL; replicating them is a
  trademark problem regardless of how the geometry was made.
- **Name-adjacency is a real risk here.** "Football Fusion", "Basketball: Zero",
  "Blue Lock", "Azure Latch" are established identities in this exact genre. A
  title or logo that reads as a sibling of any of them invites a takedown and,
  worse, makes ALLSTAR WORLD look derivative at the moment it most needs to look
  new.
- **"DRIP STORE" and "Create-a-Club" need original visual identity.** Club
  creation that lets players assemble something resembling a real club's crest or
  kit moves your trademark exposure onto *your* platform, since you would be
  hosting it. Ship original crest primitives and moderate user-made marks.
- The original route for a realistic football game is **your own league fiction**:
  invented teams, cities, crests and a season structure you own outright. That is
  also the only version of this where Create-a-Club and a Season Pass are assets
  rather than liabilities — you cannot sell cosmetics on top of someone else's
  trade dress.

### Pillars this market read implies

1. Legible on a phone in one match.
2. The same outcome on both screens — server-authoritative possession as the
   headline feature, not an implementation detail.
3. A weekly ship cadence, treated as the retention system.
4. Original league identity, with cosmetic identity as the earner.
5. Football until it holds a ranked population. Then soccer.
