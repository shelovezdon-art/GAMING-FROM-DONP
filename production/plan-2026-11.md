# ALLSTAR WORLD — plan, November 2026

Built from the 2 November audit (`docs/audits/2026-11-02/`). Every item traces to a measured finding, not a guess.

**How this is ordered.** Security first, because two exploits are live. Then *reconnection* — a surprising amount of
finished work is switched off, and reconnecting it is the cheapest quality you will ever buy. Then the football core,
because that is the market opening. World cleanup runs alongside, because it is mostly deletion and costs no design
time. Platform decisions that could invalidate work sit at the top of their sections so they are made before the work
starts, not after.

**Rule for this month:** no new venues, no new modes, no new systems until the football core is playable end to end.
The audit found three football surfaces, two soccer pitches and a hockey rink against a decision for three venues —
the failure mode here is breadth, not depth.

## 1. This week — close what is open

### 1.1 Finish Stage 3 install
`TackleService` and `TackleClient` still need to go in, **disabled**. Enabling them also needs the 12-line seam in
`PracticeService` (`ServerStorage.AWPracticeBall`, three verbs: `down`, `fumble`, `award`). Without the seam the
system decides and reports but cannot blow the whistle — fine for a first look, not for play.
*Blocked on:* nothing. Quickest path is enabling **Allow HTTP Requests** in Experience Settings so Studio pulls the
files from this repo directly.

### 1.2 The two remaining client-trust problems
- **`HoopsService` shot meter** — same flaw as the 3v3, and worse (a perfect shot is a guaranteed make). Currently
  unreachable because the 1v1 has no court. **Decide: retire the script or patch it.** Retiring is the audit's
  recommendation; it is 1,360 lines of live code that looks like a working feature.
- **Soccer shot meter** — `SoccerService:1548` trusts `d.held` with no server anchor at all. The server never sees
  the charge start, so this needs a `shootStart` intent before it can be bounded. Half a day.

### 1.3 Verify the timestamp assumption
`GetServerTimeNow()` already applies about half the player's ping. Any code that stamps a client action with it and
*then* rewinds by ping double-counts. Check `PracticeService.clientTime`, `Actions:pressTime`, and the new
`TackleService`. Roblox's own docs were wrong on this and never corrected.

### 1.4 Crowd: the last two bakes
Arms and `Base` are done. Still open:
- **Faces.** 845 of 852 fans have only a `Calm` frame; none have `Shout` or `Upset`. This is art, not a bake —
  either author the four frames or delete the face system and reclaim 852 SurfaceGuis.
- **Held props.** 702 cups and phones now follow hands that finally move. Watch the first playtest for detachment.

## 2. Next 2–3 weeks — football core to playable

**Decide first:** evaluate Roblox's first-party **Server Authority** (full release 9 July 2026, ships a Soccer
template). It supersedes hand-rolled ball networking. Costs: fixed 240 Hz physics, forced StreamingEnabled, **8
animation tracks per Animator** (a real ceiling for layered dribbling), breaking camera-input change. One hour with
the template answers whether the next months are build or adopt. **Do this before 2.1.**

### 2.1 Resolve the two-codebase split — **DECIDED, see ADR-0001**

**`TeamFootballService` is the product. The Practice Field is a test bench and is never shipped as a mode.**
The Practice Field's deterministic catch model ports across one piece at a time behind a config flag, starting with
`Receive`; the quality-roll model retires at parity. Accepted costs: Stage 3 tackling needs adapting from Practice
conventions, and TeamFootball's rules (5 possessions, TD 7, start at the 20) must be reworked to the IFAF ruleset.

#### Original framing, kept for the record
Football exists twice: the Practice Field rebuild (better engineered, no defender, no play cycle, no score) and the
older `AWFootball` 1v1/team modes (which have tackling, moves, snap cadence, routes, jump balls, downs, an AI QB).
Two catch models, two speed scales, three gravities. **Pick the Practice Field's deterministic model** — it matches
the "I know exactly why I lost" pillar — and port the play cycle across rather than maintaining both.

### 2.2 Stage 4: the play cycle
Ready → snap → live → whistle → spot, downs, score, possession swap. The rules already exist in
`AWFootball.Config.rules` (5 possessions each, 4 downs, TD 7, start at the 20, OT 1 each max 3 rounds) — those answer
four of the seven open spec questions. **No first downs exist anywhere**; decide whether that is the arcade rule or
an omission.

### 2.3 Stage 5: route practice and jump balls
`Contest` is installed and tested. Route practice is the cheapest mode to finish and the best onboarding you have.

### 2.4 Athlete bodies
Point the football services at `AWSoccer.Athlete` (lean Rthro build, real legs, kit colours, crest). Near drop-in,
and it de-risks Mixamo by proving `AthleteMotion` survives a body swap. **Then** Mixamo, where the rig is the whole
risk: Roblox wants exactly 15 meshes, 15 R15 bones, 4 influences per vertex, 19 named attachment points, 10,742
triangles. A non-conforming rig makes athletes go stiff **with no error at all**. Retarget in Blender first, and
hoist the five hardcoded animation IDs into one module before swapping anything.

## 4. Basketball — after football is playable

- **Port the half-court rules from `AW_Archive.Hoops3Service_halfcourt`** (1,233 lines, previously working). The live
  3v3 is hard-coded full-court. Everything else here depends on this.
- **Reinstate two courts from `AW_Archive.Courts_preReno2026`** into one outdoor park. The aesthetic is already right.
- **Dribble input, not mechanics.** Hand-to-floor is genuinely built and good (`Dribble` + `DribbleLook` + `BallView`,
  two-bone IK, per-move steal exposure). What is missing: a touch-drag flick reader, movement-relative flick
  direction, a dedicated speed-dribble button, and the ankle-breaker that the 3v3 lost from the 1v1.
- **Build on the Input Action System**, not `ContextActionService` — it is flagged for deprecation and your
  `PracticeClient` already uses it.
- **The rim is correct — measured 8.714 studs above the floor = 3.050 m regulation.** The audit's "1.46 studs too
  low" was wrong; it measured against the court's built scale instead of the code's conversion. The dunk gates need
  no retuning. The real issue is the reverse: the **court and backboard are ~22% oversized** relative to the players
  and the rim (`CourtSurface` 98.23 × 52.25 studs is regulation only at 3.5 studs/m, while the code converts at
  2.857). Decide whether to rescale the court; leave the rim alone.
- **Consolidate onto `Play3`/`Tuning3`** and retire the 1v1's rules stack.

## 5. Soccer — extend, do not rebuild

The audit is unambiguous: 9–15 days to extend versus 25–40 to rebuild, and a rebuild risks the parts that are
currently right. Close-control touches and the low behind-player camera **already exist and work**.

- Fix the inverted stud constant first: `Practice.S` is studs-per-metre, `Soccer.STUD` is metres-per-stud, both
  called `S` at call sites. Twenty minutes, and it will bite someone otherwise.
- Tuning to reach the reference feel: shielding / back-to-goal, imperfect first touch, camera shoulder offset and
  FOV change. 3–5 days total.
- Add the `rev` snapshot counter the football rebuild uses.
- Later: lift soccer's `Touch` into a shared module both sports use, rather than rewriting soccer to look like
  football.

## 7. Deliberately not doing

- **The arrival trench.** The most art-directed people area in the place, serving a campus already decided against.
  54 photographers that never move, 50 fans never animated at all. Gate the flash loop to a nearby client and stop.
- **More fan detail.** A frozen, perfectly-aligned crowd reads as cardboard at any polygon count. Motion plus ±15°
  of yaw jitter buys more than geometry.
- **New venues of any kind** until football is playable.
- **Monetization code.** None exists in any sport. Correct for now — DRIP STORE, Season Pass and Clubs are M08–M11,
  and the audit confirms nothing purchasable touches any gameplay value today. Keep it that way.
- **Chasing realism as the differentiator.** The top sports game on the platform wins on two buttons; the one known
  for realistic animation sits at a quarter of its players. The open space is realistic fidelity on a control scheme
  you can read instantly.

## 8. Market context

Football Fusion 2 — 1.71 billion visits, once 49,999 concurrent — is at **1,635 concurrent and eight months without
an update**. That is the opening, and it is why football goes first. Platform-wide, Roblox DAU is down 9% year on
year and bookings 12%, so plan on taking share rather than riding growth. Median retention is D1 10.3%, D30 0.5%,
with 3.8% of players ever paying — set expectations against those, not against the 2021 numbers.
